Recruitment Communication

Overview

Clear communication is an important part of the recruitment process.

Candidate Communication

Recruiters may communicate with candidates about:

* Application updates
* Interview scheduling
* Interview instructions
* Additional information
* Next steps
* Recruitment outcomes

Example Interview Invitation

Subject: Interview Invitation

Hello [Candidate Name],

Thank you for your application for the [Job Title] position.

We would like to invite you to an interview to discuss your experience and suitability for the role.

Interview Date: [Date]

Time: [Time]

Format: [Video/Phone/In-person]

Please confirm your availability.

Best regards,
Recruitment Team

Communication Principles

Recruitment communication should be:

* Clear
* Professional
* Respectful
* Timely
* Accurate

Outcome

This project demonstrates my ability to support professional candidate communication throughout the recruitment process.
