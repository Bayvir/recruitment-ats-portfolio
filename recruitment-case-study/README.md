Recruitment Case Study

Role

Customer Support Representative

Recruitment Objective

Identify and evaluate candidates for a customer support position using a structured recruitment process.

Step 1: Understand the Role

Key requirements:

* Strong written and verbal communication
* Customer service skills
* Problem-solving ability
* Basic computer skills
* Ability to work independently
* Ability to use online communication tools

Step 2: Candidate Sourcing

Potential candidates would be sourced through:

* LinkedIn
* Job boards
* Professional communities
* Referrals
* Direct candidate searches

Step 3: Candidate Screening

Candidates would be reviewed against the job requirements.

Screening factors:

* Relevant experience
* Communication skills
* Customer service experience
* Technical skills
* Availability

Step 4: Shortlisting

Candidates who meet the key requirements would be shortlisted for an interview.

Step 5: Interview

Shortlisted candidates would be asked structured behavioral and situational questions.

Example:

“Tell me about a time you dealt with a difficult customer. How did you handle the situation?”

Step 6: Evaluation

Interview results would be recorded using a standardized scorecard covering:

* Communication
* Relevant experience
* Problem solving
* Technical skills
* Teamwork

Step 7: Final Recommendation

The candidate with the strongest evidence against the job requirements would progress to the next stage.

Recruitment Tools Demonstrated

* Candidate sourcing
* CV screening
* ATS workflow
* Structured interviews
* Interview scorecards
* Candidate tracking
* Recruitment communication

Outcome

This case study demonstrates my ability to apply a structured recruitment workflow from understanding a job requirement through sourcing, screening, interviewing, evaluation, and candidate progression.
