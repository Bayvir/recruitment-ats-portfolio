Recruitment Metrics

Overview

Recruitment metrics help recruiters monitor the hiring process and identify areas that may need improvement.

Key Metrics

Time to Hire

The amount of time it takes to move a candidate from application to hiring.

Time to Fill

The amount of time it takes to fill an open position.

Application-to-Interview Rate

The percentage of applicants who progress to the interview stage.

Interview-to-Hire Rate

The percentage of interviewed candidates who receive an offer or are hired.

Source of Hire

Tracks where successful candidates came from, such as job boards, referrals, social media, or direct sourcing.

Example

Metric	Example
Applications	100
Screened	30
Interviews	10
Offers	3
Hires	2

Why Metrics Matter

Recruitment metrics can help identify bottlenecks, compare sourcing channels, monitor recruitment efficiency, and support data-informed hiring decisions.
