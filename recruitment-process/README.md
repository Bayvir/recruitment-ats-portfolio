End-to-End Recruitment Process

Recruitment Workflow

1. Understand the Role

Review the job description and identify the required skills and qualifications.

2. Source Candidates

Search for qualified candidates through job boards, professional networks, databases, referrals, and other appropriate channels.

3. Screen Applications

Compare candidate profiles against the requirements of the role.

4. Shortlist Candidates

Select candidates who meet the relevant requirements for further evaluation.

5. Conduct Interviews

Use structured questions to assess experience, skills, communication, and problem-solving ability.

6. Evaluate Candidates

Record interview results using a consistent scorecard.

7. Final Review

Compare the documented candidate information and interview results against the role requirements.

8. Offer and Hiring

Support communication with the selected candidate and completion of the required hiring steps.

9. Maintain Records

Keep candidate information and recruitment records organized throughout the process.

Outcome

This project demonstrates my understanding of the recruitment process from sourcing through candidate evaluation and hiring.
