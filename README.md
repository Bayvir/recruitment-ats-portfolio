# recruitment-ats-portfolio
Recruitment operations portfolio demonstrating candidate sourcing, ATS workflow, candidate screening, structured interviewing, and recruitment analytics.
Recruiting Portfolio Projects

Candidate Sourcing

Demonstrates candidate research, sourcing strategies, and identification of potential candidates.

Interview Techniques

Demonstrates structured interviewing, behavioral questions, STAR-method evaluation, and candidate assessment.

ATS Recruitment Workflow

Demonstrates understanding of candidate pipelines, recruitment stages, and applicant tracking systems.

Candidate Screening

Demonstrates structured CV screening and matching candidates against job requirements.

Job Description Analysis

Demonstrates the ability to analyze job descriptions and identify recruitment criteria.

Recruitment Metrics

Demonstrates understanding of basic recruitment analytics and hiring funnel metrics.

Recruitment Communication

Demonstrates professional communication with candidates throughout the recruitment process.

End-to-End Recruitment Process

Demonstrates understanding of the recruitment workflow from job requirements and sourcing through screening, interviews, evaluation, and hiring.
