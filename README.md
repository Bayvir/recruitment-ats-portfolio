About This Portfolio

This portfolio demonstrates practical knowledge of the recruitment process, including candidate sourcing, screening, structured interviewing, ATS workflows, candidate tracking, recruitment communication, and recruitment metrics.

It also includes a simulated end-to-end recruitment case study demonstrating how I would approach a hiring requirement from candidate sourcing through interview evaluation and final recommendation.
