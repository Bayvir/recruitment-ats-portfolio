Interview Example

Role

Customer Support Representative

Interview Question

Tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult customer. How did you handle the situation?

What I Look For

* Clear communication
* Patience and professionalism
* Problem-solving ability
* Ability to understand the customer’s needs
* A clear outcome

STAR Evaluation

Situation: Did the candidate clearly explain the situation?

Task: Did they explain their responsibility?

Action: Did they explain what they personally did?

Result: Did they explain the outcome?

Evaluation

The candidate should be assessed based on the quality and relevance of their answer rather than personal assumptions.

Recommendation

Use the interview scorecard to record the assessment and determine whether the candidate should proceed to the next stage.
