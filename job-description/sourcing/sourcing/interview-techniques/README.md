Interview Techniques

Overview

This project demonstrates my understanding of structured interviews and candidate evaluation.

Skills Demonstrated

* Structured interview planning
* Behavioral interview questions
* Situational interview questions
* Candidate evaluation
* Interview scorecards
* STAR method
* Interview documentation

Behavioral Interview Question

“Tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult customer or colleague. What did you do?”

Situational Interview Question

“You have several urgent tasks with competing deadlines. How would you prioritize them?”

STAR Method

* Situation — What was happening?
* Task — What was your responsibility?
* Action — What did you do?
* Result — What happened?

Candidate Evaluation

Candidates can be evaluated based on:

* Communication
* Relevant experience
* Problem solving
* Technical skills
* Teamwork
* Overall suitability for the role

Interview Scorecard

A standardized scorecard can be used to evaluate candidates consistently and document interview results.

Outcome

This project demonstrates my ability to support a structured recruitment interview process, from preparing questions to evaluating candidates and documenting interview outcomes.
